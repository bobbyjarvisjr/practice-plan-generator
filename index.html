How this works
Rate yourself,
honestly.

I'm going to ask you some questions about different parts of your playing. For each one, give yourself a score out of six.

0No knowledge at all
1Started learning it, not using it yet
2Just starting to implement it
3Using it, but still thinking about it
4Fairly confident, occasionally get lost
5Confident and fluent
6Mastered across the whole neck

The questions adapt as you go. Score low on something and I won't ask you about the stuff that comes after it. So don't inflate it. A flattering answer just gets you a pathway for a player you're not.
